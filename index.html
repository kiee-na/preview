About us

A home for Korean power engineers in North America

The KIEE North America Section connects power and energy engineers in universities, national laboratories, utilities, and industry across the U.S. and Canada. We meet every July at the IEEE PES General Meeting and run online webinars with KIEE throughout the year.

More about the Section
  • ConnectPower-system professionals in Korea and North America
  • ShareTechnology and policy trends of both regions
  • GrowGraduate students and early-career engineers
  • CollaborateJoint research between Korean and U.S. institutions
