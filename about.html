About

KIEE North America Section

The North America Section of the Korean Institute of Electrical Engineers (대한전기학회 북미지회).

Who we are

The Section brings together Korean and international power engineers working across North America: university faculty and students, national laboratory researchers, utility engineers, and industry professionals.

It started as the KIEE PES North America Chapter at IEEE PES GM 2025 in Austin and was officially launched as a Section at IEEE PES GM 2026 in Montreal.

Mission

  • Connect power-system professionals in Korea and North America
  • Share technology and policy trends of both regions
  • Support graduate students and early-career engineers
  • Seed joint research between Korean and U.S. institutions

Milestones

  1. Jul 2025 · Austin, TXPreparatory meeting at IEEE PES GM 2025, 108 registered participants
  2. Nov 2025Webinar series with the KIEE Study Committee on Distributed Power Grid begins
  3. Jul 2026 · Montreal, CanadaOfficial inaugural ceremony at IEEE PES GM 2026
  4. Nov 2026 · Los AngelesCooperation meeting with KETEP
  5. Jul 2027 · San Francisco, CAAnnual meeting at IEEE PES GM 2027

What our members work on

Section members cover the full power system, from generation to the grid edge.

  • Generation and IBRsEMT modeling, grid codes, smart inverters
  • TransmissionPlanning, dynamic line rating, wide-area dynamics
  • SubstationsIEC 61850 automation, protection, digital substations
  • DistributionDER integration, outage prediction, utility analytics
  • Microgrids and EVsBESS, EV charging, resilience
  • Data centersLarge-load integration, ride-through, flexibility
  • AI for the gridGrid foundation models, control room AI, digital twins
  • CybersecurityOT, ICCP, IBR and EV charging security
