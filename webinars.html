Co-hosted with the KIEE Study Committee on Distributed Power Grid and held on Zoom for members in Korea and North America.

Webinar 5Nov 13, 2026Korea time

Cyber Security of Control Room of the Future using Artificial Intelligence

Alexandru Ștefanov · TU Delft, The Netherlands

  • Grid cyber attacks in Ukraine (2015, 2016, 2022) as advanced persistent threats (APTs)
  • APTs on OT networks in control rooms and digital substations can cause cascading failures
  • AI-based spatio-temporal APT detection and correlation for situational awareness
  • Cyber range for joint attack/defense exercises of grid operators and SOC teams
Webinar 4May 28, 2026Korea time

Security for Electric Vehicle Charging Ecosystem

Myungsoo Jun · National Laboratory of the Rockies

  • EV charging growth shifts power system operation and adds new interconnections
  • Cybersecurity issues in charging infrastructure: data integrity, secure communication, resilience
  • Machine learning-based anomaly detection for charging systems
  • Security and vulnerability assessment of EV ecosystem mobile applications
Webinar 3Apr 17, 2026Korea time

Analysis and Mitigation of AI Datacenter-Induced Wide-Area Grid Oscillation

Min-Seung Ko · University of Texas at Austin

  • AI data centers behave very differently from conventional industrial loads
  • Workload-driven power fluctuations can excite local and inter-area oscillations
  • Effects of location, fluctuation frequency, and grid modal characteristics
  • Mitigation: power smoothing with co-located hybrid storage (ESS + supercapacitor)
Webinar 2Dec 18, 2025Korea time

Advances in Future Grid Control Rooms

Seong Lok Choi · National Laboratory of the Rockies

  • Operators face data overload and need faster decisions with more DERs and variable generation
  • AI, digital twins, and visualization integrated into next-generation control rooms
  • Proof-of-concept demos: alarm analysis, contingency evaluation, redispatch support, simulation studies
  • Natural-language workflows for briefing and automated studies, within cyber and compliance limits
Webinar 1Nov 5, 2025Korea time

EMT Modeling and Simulation of Large-scale Inverter-based Resources (IBRs) with High Granularity

Jongchan Choi · Oak Ridge National Laboratory

  • Large-scale IBRs are changing grid dynamics and disturbance response
  • NERC event reports show partial tripping and unintended power reduction in IBR plants after disturbances
  • High-fidelity EMT models that represent individual inverters inside a plant are needed to explain such events
  • Modeling challenges for large IBR plants and EMT techniques that address them
